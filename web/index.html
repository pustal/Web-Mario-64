Game

The game is made on the TV from your own Super Mario 64 ROM (USA or Japan; .z64, .n64 or .v64, 8 MB). The TV turns it into the game's archive once, which takes a minute or two. The ROM can also come from a USB drive: a folder named Super Mario 64, or RetroArch's roms/n64 folder (a file with "mario" in its name).

Mods

Mods are .o2r or .otr archives (texture packs, models, music), made for Ghostship. They can stay on a USB drive, in Super Mario 64/mods. The game loads them when it starts; turn them on or off in Ghostship's menu (BACK on the remote, then Game settings).

Saves

The game's save files (the four save slots) with its settings and controls. A backup is a small zip file: keep it, or restore it on another TV. Restoring while the game runs takes effect when it starts again.

Trouble?

The game crashed at the last launch: its logs are the "last launch" ones below. The TV starts the game again when OK is pressed, or after 30 seconds.

The logs help when reporting a problem. The game's log; the app's start-up log: this launch, the last launch.
